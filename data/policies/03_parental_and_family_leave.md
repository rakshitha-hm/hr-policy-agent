# Parental, Care and Family Leave Policy

**Company:** Northwind Analytics B.V.
**Policy owner:** People Team
**Applies to:** all employees in the Netherlands, Germany and India
**Effective from:** 1 January 2026

## 1. Purpose

Northwind supports employees when their family grows or needs care. This policy describes parental, adoption, care and bereavement leave. Where local law is more generous, local law applies.

## 2. Eligibility

You are eligible for the fully paid company leave in this policy after 6 months of continuous employment. Employees with less than 6 months of service receive the statutory leave of their country.

## 3. Birth parent leave

- 16 weeks of fully paid leave (100% of salary).
- Leave can start up to 6 weeks before the expected due date.
- Notify your manager and the People Team at least 8 weeks before the planned start.

## 4. Non-birth parent leave

- 6 weeks of fully paid leave (100% of salary).
- Must be taken within the first 6 months after the birth or adoption.
- Can be taken in one block or in up to 3 blocks of at least 1 week.

## 5. Adoption and foster leave

- The primary carer receives 16 weeks of fully paid leave, the same as birth parent leave.
- The secondary carer receives 6 weeks of fully paid leave, the same as non-birth parent leave.
- The leave starts from the date the child is placed with the family.

## 6. Return to work

- After parental leave you return to the same role, or an equivalent role with the same pay and level.
- In the first 4 weeks after returning, you may work up to 20% fewer hours on full pay to ease back in.

## 7. Short-term care leave

- Up to 5 paid days per calendar year to care for a sick child, partner or parent.
- Register it in PeopleHub as "care leave". No medical certificate is needed.

## 8. Bereavement leave

| Relationship | Paid bereavement leave |
| --- | --- |
| Partner, child or parent | 5 working days |
| Sibling, grandparent or parent-in-law | 3 working days |
| Other relatives or close friends | 1 working day |

Bereavement leave does not reduce annual leave and does not need advance approval. Inform your manager as soon as you can.

## 9. Unpaid leave

Employees with at least 12 months of service may request up to 3 months of unpaid leave once every 3 years, for example for a sabbatical. Unpaid leave must be approved by your manager and the People Team at least 2 months in advance.
