# Code of Conduct

**Company:** Northwind Analytics B.V.
**Policy owner:** Legal and Compliance
**Applies to:** all employees, contractors and interns
**Effective from:** 1 January 2026

## 1. Our values

We act with integrity, treat everyone with respect and protect the data our clients trust us with.

## 2. Respect and inclusion

- Discrimination, harassment and bullying are not tolerated, in the office, online or at company events.
- This includes discrimination based on gender, age, ethnicity, religion, disability, sexual orientation or any other personal characteristic.

## 3. Gifts and hospitality

- You may accept gifts or hospitality from clients or suppliers worth up to EUR 50 (INR 4,000) per person per year.
- Gifts above this value must be declined or reported to Legal and Compliance, who decide what happens to them.
- Cash or cash-equivalent gifts (such as gift cards) may never be accepted.

## 4. Conflicts of interest

- Declare any situation where your personal interests could conflict with Northwind's, for example a side job, an investment in a supplier or a close relative working for a client.
- Declare conflicts in PeopleHub under "Declarations" within 30 days of the situation arising.
- Side jobs are allowed if they do not compete with Northwind, do not use company resources and are declared.

## 5. Data protection and confidentiality

- Handle personal data according to the GDPR and Northwind's data protection policy.
- Share client and employee data only with people who need it for their work.
- Report a suspected data breach to the Data Protection Officer at dpo@northwind-analytics.example within 24 hours.

## 6. Speaking up

- Report suspected breaches of this code to your manager, to Legal and Compliance, or anonymously through the Speak Up line.
- Northwind does not tolerate retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.

## 7. Consequences

Breaching this code can lead to disciplinary action, up to and including dismissal.
