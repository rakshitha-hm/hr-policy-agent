# Travel and Expenses Policy

**Company:** Northwind Analytics B.V.
**Policy owner:** Finance Team
**Applies to:** all employees
**Effective from:** 1 January 2026

## 1. Principles

Spend company money as if it were your own. Expenses must be reasonable, necessary for your work and supported by a receipt.

## 2. Submitting expense claims

- Submit claims in PeopleHub within 30 days of the expense. Claims submitted later than 90 days after the expense are rejected.
- Attach an itemised receipt for every expense above EUR 10 (INR 800).
- Reimbursement is paid with the next monthly salary after approval.

## 3. Approval limits

| Claim amount (per claim) | Approver |
| --- | --- |
| Up to EUR 1,000 (INR 80,000) | Your manager |
| Above EUR 1,000 (INR 80,000) | Your manager and your department director |
| Above EUR 5,000 (INR 400,000) | Manager, director and the Finance Director |

## 4. Travel

### 4.1 Booking
- Book business travel through the company travel tool at least 14 days in advance where possible.
- Train is preferred over flying for trips under 500 km within Europe.

### 4.2 Flights
- Economy class for flights up to 6 hours.
- Premium economy for flights longer than 6 hours, with director approval.
- Business class is not reimbursed.

### 4.3 Hotels
| City type | Maximum per night |
| --- | --- |
| Amsterdam, Berlin, London, Paris | EUR 200 |
| Other European cities | EUR 150 |
| Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi | INR 9,000 |

## 5. Meals during business travel

The daily meal limit on travel days is:

| Country of travel | Daily meal limit |
| --- | --- |
| Netherlands or Germany | EUR 60 |
| India | INR 3,000 |

Alcohol is not reimbursed, except at client dinners approved in advance by your director.

## 6. Mileage

Using your own car for business travel is reimbursed at EUR 0.23 per km in the Netherlands and EUR 0.30 per km in Germany. Commuting between home and your normal office is not reimbursed.

## 7. Non-reimbursable expenses

- Traffic or parking fines
- Personal entertainment and minibar
- Upgrades for personal comfort
- Lost personal items

## 8. Misuse

Submitting false or inflated claims is a serious breach of the Code of Conduct and may lead to dismissal.
