# Sick Leave Policy

**Company:** Northwind Analytics B.V.
**Policy owner:** People Team
**Applies to:** all employees in the Netherlands, Germany and India
**Effective from:** 1 January 2026

## 1. Purpose

If you are ill, your health comes first. This policy explains how to report sickness, when you need a medical certificate and how you are paid while sick.

## 2. Reporting sickness

- Inform your manager by phone or chat before 10:00 on your first day of sickness, and register the sickness in PeopleHub the same day.
- You do not need to share your diagnosis or medical details with your manager.
- Register your recovery in PeopleHub on the day you return to work.

## 3. Medical certificate

| Country | When a medical certificate is required |
| --- | --- |
| Netherlands | Not required. If you are sick for more than 7 calendar days, the company doctor (arbodienst) contacts you. |
| Germany | From the 4th calendar day of sickness |
| India | From the 4th consecutive working day of sickness |

Upload the certificate in PeopleHub. Only the People Team can see it.

## 4. Pay during sickness

| Country | Pay during sickness |
| --- | --- |
| Netherlands | 100% of salary for the first 52 weeks (statutory minimum is 70%; Northwind tops this up to 100%), then 70% in the second year |
| Germany | 100% of salary for the first 6 weeks per illness; after that, statutory sickness benefit from the health insurer |
| India | 12 days of paid sick leave per calendar year at 100% of salary; further sick days are taken from annual leave or as unpaid leave |

Unused paid sick days in India do not carry over to the next year and are not paid out.

## 5. Long-term sickness

If you are sick for more than 4 consecutive weeks, the People Team and your manager agree a return-to-work plan with you. In the Netherlands this follows the Gatekeeper Improvement Act (Wet verbetering poortwachter), including a plan of approach in week 8.

## 6. Sickness during annual leave

If you become sick during annual leave, report it as described in section 2. The sick days are converted from annual leave to sick leave and returned to your annual leave balance, provided you report it on the first day of sickness.

## 7. Caring for a sick family member

Short-term care leave for a sick child, partner or parent is covered by the Care and Family Leave Policy, not by this sick leave policy.
