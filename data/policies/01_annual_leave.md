# Annual Leave Policy

**Company:** Northwind Analytics B.V.
**Policy owner:** People Team
**Applies to:** all employees in the Netherlands, Germany and India
**Effective from:** 1 January 2026

## 1. Purpose

Northwind wants every employee to take real time off to rest and recharge. This policy explains how much paid annual leave you receive, how to request it and what happens to unused days.

## 2. Annual leave entitlement

Full-time employees receive the following paid annual leave per calendar year, based on the country of their employment contract:

| Country | Base entitlement (full-time) |
| --- | --- |
| Netherlands | 25 working days |
| Germany | 28 working days |
| India | 21 working days |

### 2.1 Long-service bonus days

Employees receive 1 extra day of annual leave for every 5 full years of service, up to a maximum of 5 extra days per year. Years of service are counted from your hire date to 1 January of the leave year.

Example: an employee in the Netherlands who joined on 1 March 2015 has 10 full years of service on 1 January 2026 and receives 25 + 2 = 27 days in 2026.

### 2.2 Part-time employees

Part-time employees receive annual leave pro rata to their contracted hours (FTE). An employee in Germany working 0.8 FTE receives 28 x 0.8 = 22.4 days, rounded up to the nearest half day (22.5 days).

### 2.3 New joiners and leavers

If you join or leave during the year, your entitlement for that year is calculated pro rata to the number of full months worked, rounded up to the nearest half day.

## 3. Requesting annual leave

- Request leave in PeopleHub, the HR portal, before it starts.
- For leave of more than 3 consecutive working days, request it at least 2 weeks in advance.
- For leave of more than 10 consecutive working days, request it at least 6 weeks in advance.
- Your manager approves or rejects the request within 5 working days. If your manager is absent, the request goes to their manager.
- Leave can be taken in full days or half days.

Managers may only reject a request for a clear business reason, such as a critical release date or too many people from the same team being absent. A rejected request must include a reason in PeopleHub.

## 4. Carry-over of unused leave

- You may carry over a maximum of 5 unused days of annual leave into the next calendar year.
- Carried-over days must be used by 31 March of the next year. Any carried-over days not used by 31 March expire.
- Days above the 5-day limit expire on 31 December, unless your manager and the People Team approved an exception in writing, for example because a project prevented you from taking leave.
- Statutory minimum leave rules in your country always apply where they are more generous than this policy.

## 5. Payment of unused leave

Unused annual leave is not paid out during employment. When employment ends, any remaining annual leave for the current year is paid out in the final salary, in line with local law.

## 6. Public holidays

Public holidays are in addition to annual leave. Each office follows its local public holiday calendar:

| Office | Public holidays per year |
| --- | --- |
| Amsterdam | 9 |
| Berlin | 10 |
| Bengaluru | 12 |
