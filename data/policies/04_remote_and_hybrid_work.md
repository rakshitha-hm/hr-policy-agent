# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

**Company:** Northwind Analytics B.V.
**Policy owner:** People Team and Workplace Team
**Applies to:** all employees
**Effective from:** 1 January 2026

## 1. Work modes

Every employee has one of three work modes, recorded in PeopleHub:

| Work mode | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Office | Works from the office every working day |
| Hybrid | Works from the office at least 2 days per week, the rest from home |
| Remote | Works from home and visits the office only for planned team events |

Your work mode is agreed with your manager and the People Team. Changing it requires a request in PeopleHub and approval from both.

## 2. Home office set-up allowance

A one-time allowance to set up a safe and ergonomic home workspace:

| Country | One-time home office allowance |
| --- | --- |
| Netherlands | EUR 500 |
| Germany | EUR 500 |
| India | INR 25,000 |

**Eligibility:**
- Your work mode is hybrid or remote, and
- You have completed 3 months of employment (your probation period).

Office-based employees are not eligible. Claim the allowance as an expense in the category "home_office" with receipts, within 6 months of becoming eligible. The allowance can be claimed only once during your employment.

## 3. Monthly internet allowance

Remote employees receive a monthly internet allowance, paid with salary:

| Country | Monthly internet allowance |
| --- | --- |
| Netherlands | EUR 30 |
| Germany | EUR 30 |
| India | INR 1,000 |

Hybrid and office employees do not receive the monthly internet allowance.

## 4. Working from another country

- You may work from a country other than your contract country for up to 20 working days per calendar year.
- Each period abroad must be approved in advance by your manager and the People Team, because of tax and immigration rules.
- Working from abroad is not allowed during your probation period.
- Working from a country under international sanctions is never allowed.

## 5. Equipment

Northwind provides a laptop, a monitor and a headset to all employees. Equipment remains company property and must be returned when employment ends.

## 6. Security at home

- Always use the company VPN when working outside the office.
- Lock your screen when you leave your laptop.
- Do not let family members or other people use company equipment.
- Report a lost or stolen device to the IT service desk within 24 hours.
