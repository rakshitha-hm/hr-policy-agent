# Learning and Development Policy

**Company:** Northwind Analytics B.V.
**Policy owner:** People Team
**Applies to:** all employees who have completed probation
**Effective from:** 1 January 2026

## 1. Purpose

Northwind invests in your growth. This policy describes the learning budget, study days and conditions for repaying training costs.

## 2. Annual learning budget

Each employee has a personal learning budget per calendar year:

| Country | Annual learning budget |
| --- | --- |
| Netherlands | EUR 1,500 |
| Germany | EUR 1,500 |
| India | INR 60,000 |

- Part-time employees receive the full budget, not a pro rata amount.
- The budget can be used for courses, certifications, conferences, books and online learning platforms related to your current or a realistic future role.
- Unused budget does not carry over to the next year.
- New employees can use the budget after completing their 3-month probation period.

Claim learning costs as an expense in the category "training".

## 3. Study days

- Employees receive up to 3 paid study days per calendar year for exams or course attendance.
- Request study days in PeopleHub as "study leave" at least 2 weeks in advance.

## 4. Larger programmes

Programmes costing more than the annual budget (for example a part-time master's degree) can be requested through your manager. They need approval from your department director and the People Team, based on business relevance.

## 5. Repayment clause

If a single course or programme costs more than EUR 2,000 (INR 150,000), you agree to a repayment clause:

| Leaving the company within | Share of the cost you repay |
| --- | --- |
| 12 months after completing the course | 100% |
| 12 to 24 months after completing the course | 50% |
| More than 24 months after completing the course | 0% |

No repayment applies if Northwind ends your employment, for example in a redundancy.

## 6. Mentoring and internal learning

All employees can join the internal mentoring programme and the monthly Learning Friday sessions. These do not use your learning budget.
