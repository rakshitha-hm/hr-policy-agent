# Working Hours and Overtime Policy

**Company:** Northwind Analytics B.V.
**Policy owner:** People Team
**Applies to:** all employees
**Effective from:** 1 January 2026

## 1. Standard working hours

- A full-time working week is 40 hours, Monday to Friday.
- Part-time hours are set in your contract as an FTE value (for example 0.8 FTE = 32 hours).
- Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time. Outside core hours you may plan your own working time, in agreement with your team.

## 2. Breaks and rest

- Take a break of at least 30 minutes when you work more than 5.5 hours in a day.
- Keep at least 11 hours of rest between two working days.
- Working on Sundays and public holidays requires prior approval from your manager.

## 3. Overtime

Overtime means hours worked above your contracted hours at your manager's request.

| Job level | Overtime compensation |
| --- | --- |
| L1 to L3 | Time off in lieu: 1 hour off for each overtime hour, or 1.5 hours off for overtime on weekends and public holidays |
| L4 to L6 | No overtime compensation; working hours are flexible and included in salary |

- Overtime must be approved by your manager in advance and registered in PeopleHub.
- Time off in lieu must be taken within 3 months, otherwise it expires.
- Overtime may not exceed 8 hours per week averaged over 4 weeks.

## 4. On-call duty

Engineers on the on-call rota receive EUR 150 (INR 6,000) per on-call week, plus time off in lieu for hours actually worked during incidents, regardless of job level.

## 5. Probation period

- New employees have a probation period of 3 months.
- During probation, either side can end employment with 1 week of notice.

## 6. Notice periods

| Situation | Notice period |
| --- | --- |
| Employee resigns, after probation | 1 month (Netherlands, Germany), 2 months (India) |
| Company ends employment | In line with local law and the employment contract |
